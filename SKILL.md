## Commands & Workflows

### 1. Script Writing (/yt-script [Channel Name] [Raw Idea / Topic])
- Hook Generation: Raw idea milne par 21 proven hook formulas (Curiosity Gap, Negative Hook, Visual Hook, Bold Claim, etc.) ka use karke hooks generate karein.
- Hook Scoring: Har hook ko 1 se 100 ke scale par score karein (Retention, Curiosity, aur CTR potential ke basis par) aur sabse best hook highlight karein.
- Full Script Draft: Selected hook ke baad selected channel ke tone ke mutabiq detailed video script likhein jisme visual cues, pacing, aur clear call-to-action (CTA) shaamil ho.

### 2. Packaging (/yt-package [Channel Name] [Video Topic / Concept])
- Title Optimization: High-CTR aur SEO-friendly titles ke 5-10 options dein jo us channel ki audience ko attract karein.
- Thumbnail Concepts: Visual thumbnail concepts design karein (Foreground focus, text overlay, color contrast, aur emotion triggers).
- Synergy Check: Title aur thumbnail ka combination test karein ke dono mil kar ek strong story clickbait banayein bina viewer ko mislead kiye.

### 3. Retention Analysis (/yt-retention [Retention Graph Data / Script Drop Points])
- Drop-off Identification: Script ya viewer retention data mein wo spots highlight karein jahan audience ke chhod kar jaane (drop off) ka risk sabse zyada hai.
- Fixes & Pacing: Un boring parts ko replace karne ke liye quick pacing fixes, pattern interrupts, sound cues, ya visual transitions suggest karein.

### 4. Shorts Cutter (/yt-shorts [Long Video Script / Topic])
- Bite-Sized Moments: Long video se sabse engaging, high-drama, ya valuable 30-60 second ke parts extract karein.
- Shorts Script Format: Fast-paced short-form script layout banayein jisme pehle 3 seconds mein solid hook ho (Shorts/Reels platforms ke liye).
